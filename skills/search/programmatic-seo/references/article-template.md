# Article Generation Template

In [`assets/article-template.md`](../assets/article-template.md), the full frontmatter and body skeleton for a programmatic blog article.

## Content Brief Inputs

- Target keyword (primary)
- Secondary keywords (3-5)
- Search intent
- Target word count (1500-2500)
- Competitor articles to beat
- Unique angle

## Image Guidance

**Stock:** Unsplash (`unsplash.com/s/photos/[topic]`), Pexels (`pexels.com/search/[topic]/`).

**Optimize:** `cwebp input.jpg -q 80 -o output.webp`.

**SEO:** descriptive filename (`topic-feature-description.webp`), alt text with keyword, dimensions 1200x630 (social) / 800px max (inline).
