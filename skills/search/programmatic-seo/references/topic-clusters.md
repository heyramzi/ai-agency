# Pillar viability and hub-and-spoke patterns

Folded in from the retired `seo-keyword-research` skill, whose `topic-cluster-templates.md` this
replaces. `content-strategy.md` points here for the pillar checklist and the four hub-and-spoke
shapes; nothing else in that file's original content earned a place here.

## Pillar viability checklist

Score a candidate pillar before writing anything under it. All five hold, or it is not a pillar:

- At least 8-12 subtopics can be identified.
- Combined cluster keyword volume exceeds 5,000/month.
- The topic is directly relevant to the product or service.
- Unique expertise or data exists to write it, not a compilation of what already ranks.
- No competitor has already built a dominant cluster on it.

## Mapping subtopics

Per pillar, table every subtopic before drafting the first page:

| # | Subtopic | Target keyword | Volume | Difficulty | Intent | Format |
| - | - | - | - | - | - | - |

The pillar links to every subtopic page; every subtopic page links back to the pillar and to 2-3
sibling subtopics. Orphan cluster pages (no inbound link) do not get discovered or valued.

## Hub-and-spoke shapes

Four patterns, picked by what the topic actually looks like rather than by default:

**Classic hub-spoke.** One pillar, spokes that do not overlap much: benefits, examples, tools,
mistakes, checklist. Fits a broad educational topic with no natural skill ladder.

**Layered cluster.** Beginner / intermediate / advanced tiers under one pillar. Fits a topic with
a real skill progression (a technical or how-to domain), where a beginner page and an advanced
page would otherwise fight for the same slot.

**Use-case cluster.** One spoke per audience segment (for SMBs, for enterprise, for agencies).
Fits a product or service with genuinely distinct buyer segments, each searching differently for
the same underlying thing.

**Process cluster.** Sequential spokes, one per step of a multi-step process, each complex enough
to carry its own page. Fits a how-to pillar whose steps do not fit in one article without padding.

Pick one shape per pillar. Mixing shapes inside one cluster is how the internal linking plan stops
making sense.
