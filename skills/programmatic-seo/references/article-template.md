# Article Generation Template

Use this template as the starting structure for any programmatic blog article.

## Frontmatter

```markdown
---
title: "Your Title with Primary Keyword"
description: "150-160 char meta with keyword and CTA"
slug: url-friendly-slug
publishedAt: 2026-01-23
updatedAt: 2026-01-23
author: Author Name
tags: ["tag1", "tag2", "tag3"]
image: /blog/images/article-hero.jpg
imageAlt: Descriptive alt text with keyword
---
```

## Body Structure

```markdown
# Your Title with Primary Keyword

**TL;DR**: [50-word summary with primary keyword]

## What Is [Topic]?
[150 words, primary keyword in first paragraph]

## Why You Need [Topic]
[Benefits, addresses search intent]

## Top Options Compared

### 1. Your Product (Our Pick)
### 2. [Competitor]

## How to Choose

| Feature | Option A | Option B | Option C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $X | $Y | $Z |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### [Question from PAA]
[Direct answer for featured snippet]

## Conclusion
[CTA]
```

## Content Brief Inputs

- Target keyword (primary)
- Secondary keywords (3-5)
- Search intent
- Target word count (1500-2500)
- Competitor articles to beat
- Unique angle

## Image Guidance

**Stock:** Unsplash (`unsplash.com/s/photos/[topic]`), Pexels (`pexels.com/search/[topic]/`).

**Optimize:** `cwebp input.jpg -q 80 -o output.webp`.

**SEO:** descriptive filename (`topic-feature-description.webp`), alt text with keyword, dimensions 1200x630 (social) / 800px max (inline).
