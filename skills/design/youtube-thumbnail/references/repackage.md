# The repackage loop

The title and the frame are the only two things that can be changed after a video ships, which
makes them the only live repair available. This is the procedure for "my CTR is low", and it runs
on the relative ranking rather than on CTR, because CTR moves with impressions and answers a
different question.

Read the video's rank out of the channel's last ten by views, then act on the band:

| Band | Read it as | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 of 10 | Outperformed its own channel | Leave it. Work out which brick did it and reuse that, per `idea-mining` |
| 4 to 6 of 10 | Middle. Usually a smaller subject, not a worse frame | Leave it. Check the subject's reach against the pillar before blaming the packaging |
| 7 to 10 of 10 | Underperformed | Repackage: new title, new frame, both at once, stating the guess for why the first pair failed |

**Say the guess out loud before changing anything**, because a repackage with no hypothesis is a
second random draw and teaches nothing either way.

**A thumbnail A/B needs a wide margin to mean anything.** A variant leading by a small amount on a
small sample is noise, and calling it early is how a house style gets built on one lucky frame.
When one variant does break clear, the next test is not that variant against two unrelated ones:
it is that variant against two frames close to it, which is what isolates the thing that worked.

**Reviving a dead video means deleting it first.** A re-upload of a video still on the channel is
matched against the original and starts suppressed. Delete the original, wait at least three days,
then upload the recut with a new title and a new frame. It is then a fresh video with no history.
This costs the original's watch history and comments permanently, so it is for a video that is
already dead, and never for one that is merely slow.

The repackage order across the whole repair, which this skill is only the last step of: subject,
then title, then thumbnail, then hook. `idea-mining` owns the first, and it is the one that moves
the number.
