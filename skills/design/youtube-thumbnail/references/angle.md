# The angle, its references, and the repackage loop

An angle is 3 things, written in this order: the claim (the sentence a viewer believes the
second they see the frame, in their words, never a description of the picture), the proof line (`payoff` in the schema: the
beat of the video that proves it, quoted), and the title the claim wants.

## The packaging is one unit

[`copy.md`](copy.md) bans a category name, a sentiment and a restatement on the frame, then step 1
took the title as given, so the law ran on 4 words and was waved through on the 60
characters beside them. The ops-ceiling video: title Why Your Agency Hits the Ops Ceiling
names a category and makes no claim, so the frame spent its words repairing it (`HIRING WON'T FIX
IT`, `SALES ISN'T THE PROBLEM`, `BEST MONTH. WORST WEEK.`). Contest the title: return the title
the angle wants, or the existing one verbatim when it already carries the claim. A title from
keyword research is optimised for a search term and says nothing a viewer would click on.

## A claim the video can't back up is a hole

This runs before the script on purpose. A claim the video can't back up ends 1 of 2 ways: it's
bait and gets cut, or the video gains the beat. Found here it costs a bullet; after the shoot, a
pickup or a lie. Quote the proof line, because a summary can't be checked and a line can.

## 3 claims, never 3 layouts

The 3 concepts (`safe read`, `owned read`, `break`) used to be 3 design strategies on one
beat: one concept rendered 3 times. 3 different claims about 3 different beats.

## Lift the claim from what he says

A claim written from the brief is a claim about a plan. A line said on camera is a promise already
paid, and arrives in his voice. The ops-ceiling take at 00:18: "Your best month, and you know it
already if you reach that, is the one where your delivery breaks." `BEST MONTH. WORST WEEK.` is
that line in 4 words. Where a script or transcript exists, quote from it.

No niche scan, no claim about the field: the 20 Aug ops-ceiling set had none and its bets ("nobody
in this SERP says that") were invented, and one decided the pick. Without a scan, a bet covers this
frame only.

## Recording the pick

Record it, don't remember it. On `/youtube/concepts` the "Run with this angle" control writes
`chosen` onto the stored set; the script generator reads it through `chosenAngle` and
`packagingBrief`(the concept schema)
. The script treats the claim as a debt: paid in the first block, in the frame's
words. Left unrecorded, the ops-ceiling video claimed "hiring won't fix it" in 3 frames and reached
it at 01:33.

What kills an angle: it restates the title; it names a category (`CEO Dashboard` is a shelf);
it can't be quoted against the video; it asserts something about competitors the run never saw; it
repeats the frame beside it in another composition.

## Sourcing references (the band sheets left the concept shapeless, or a set looks like the last)

[`evidence.md`](evidence.md) can't supply an unused shape, because every frame it measures is
already on YouTube in this category. Gather 10 to 15 references from 5 sources, because 2
references make a copy of one of them.

| Source | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Internal outliers: our frames that beat the channel average | our own slug in the band script |
| External outliers: a frame that beat its own channel's average | the 9 slugs, `sheet-winner.jpg` |
| Model channels: same format, different subject | not on disk (a woodworking build-in-public channel for a software one) |
| Bookmarks: frames that stopped the scroll, saved on sight | a private "thumbnail inspo" playlist; a frame recalled later is remembered wrong |
| Off-platform: billboards, film posters, title sequences, served ads | Frameset for film frames; the only source that hands the niche a new shape |

Don't filter by topic (it returns the frames the sheets already hold). Take the mechanism and leave
the picture: per reference, what question does it make me ask, what emotion, and how (name the
device). Transfer the device to this claim: a title-sequence frame with flat things rendered solid
was "subversion of an expectation", so draw a thumbnail that isn't flat. "It just looks good" means
unread. The mechanism feeds the template choice; one no template expresses is a new template or a
mechanism for another video.

Mechanisms read off 4 winner sheets on 28 Aug 2026:

- Matt Gray, the withheld object. `work less`, `just start`, `no one told you`: half a sentence
  whose object is missing. The location carries the emotion and the words carry the question, and never
  both on one channel. `$0 -> $10K -> $1M` shows a transformation as a sequence in one frame.
- Systems Made Better, the viewer supplies the baseline. `YOUR IPAD. BUT BETTER.` compares to
  what they own, so the gap is felt. Plus an arrow at the exact element.
- Ali Abdaal, the face is a reaction shot. He holds the notebook, the keyboard or the whiteboard;
  the hand proves the artifact is real.
- Our own bands. The winner's noun is a job, the control's a feature; the winner bounds the
  effort or scope and sets type in the top half, the control hangs it bottom-left over a screenshot.

## The repackage loop

Title and frame are the only live repair after a video ships. It runs on rank, not CTR (CTR moves
with impressions). Read the rank in the channel's last ten by views:

| Band | Do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 of 10 | Leave it. Find which brick did it and reuse that (`video-script`'s `references/choosing-ideas.md`) |
| 4 to 6 | Leave it. Usually a smaller subject, since the frame is no worse; check reach against the pillar |
| 7 to 10 | Repackage title and frame together, stating why the first pair failed |

Say the guess before changing anything: a repackage with no hypothesis is a second random draw.
A thumbnail A/B needs a wide margin: a small lead on a small sample is noise, and calling it
early builds a house style on one lucky frame. When one variant breaks clear, test it against 2
frames close to it.

Reviving a dead video means deleting it first. A re-upload of a video still on the channel is
matched to the original and starts suppressed. Delete the original, wait at least 3 days, then
upload the recut with a new title and frame. It costs the original's watch history and comments
permanently, so only for a dead video, never a slow one.
