# Concept archetypes

The general-YouTube catalogue. It holds on entertainment-scale channels, where it was
derived.

**In the founder, systems and AI-tooling niche it was measured and found partly
backwards.** [`niche-evidence.md`](niche-evidence.md) is the source of truth for this
skill: 270 frames across ten channels, each split into a winner band and a control band.
Where the two disagree, the evidence file wins. This page is kept because the vocabulary
is useful and because two of the seven do survive the control.

| Archetype | When to use | Composition | Status in this niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reaction face | Personality-driven, surprise | Face left/right, eyes to focal object, mouth open | **Refuted.** No shocked face appears in any winner band, on any of the ten channels. |
| Juxtaposition | Comparison, transformation | Split frame, two contrasting halves | **Holds**, in the negation form: crossed-out options and one survivor. Nick Puru, Michele Torti, Liam Ottley. |
| Single hero object | Product, gadget, money | Object centre-right, clean background | **Holds and is the strongest.** It is the faceless variant. Systems Made Better, Ross Harkness, Jordan Ross. |
| Before / after | Tutorial, fix, glow-up | Left bad, right good | **Holds**, as `OLD -> NEW` and `SLOP -> FIXED`. |
| Numbered stakes | Challenge, "$10k", "7 levels" | Giant number, face reacting | **Split.** A numbered strip where every cell is a different real thing wins; a ramp of generic step icons is a control marker on five channels. |
| Mystery / question | Investigative, "what's inside" | Object partly hidden, hand reaching | Not observed either way. Untested here. |
| Forbidden / danger | Risk, extreme | Red tint, caution tape, hazard | Not observed. The niche's version is the negation, above. |

Pick exactly one and name it in the build order. Blending two is the most common way a
thumbnail ends up saying nothing.

## What replaces the general advice here

The three general rules that this niche's evidence overturns, so they do not creep back
in from the playbook:

- **"The face must be 40% of frame height with one emotion at 11."** A face is not a
  lever in this niche; a close-up filling the frame is a control marker. Small in a real
  place, or holding the artifact, or absent.
- **"Saturate hard, MrBeast palette."** No max-saturation palette appears in any winner
  band here. Two working colours and a plate that fights the white UI is what wins.
- **"Numbers and dollar signs out-CTR letters."** Somebody else's money is a control
  marker on four channels. Your own zero-to-X ladder is the exception.
