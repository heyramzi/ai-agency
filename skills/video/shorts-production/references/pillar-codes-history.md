# Pillar codes: the migration, the traps and the weighting question

Moved out of SKILL.md on 25 Aug 2026 to hold it under the 250-line ceiling. The live table and the
naming rule stay in the skill; this is what the codes came from and what they still owe.

This replaced a scheme of five prefixes (`H`, `U`, `HA`, `HC`, `HM`) over eight numeric
families, which had accumulated **19 codes that each meant two different videos**. The
old families map: 1xx, 2xx, 6xx and HC to **C**; 3xx and HA to **A**; 4xx to **P**,
except the 4xx entries that were ClickUp or Make tutorials rather than our own software,
which are **C**; the personal systems in 8xx (GTD, 12 Week Year, Eisenhower, habit
tracker) to **M**, the rest of 8xx to **C**, and the agent in 8xx to **A**; HM to **S**.

**Two traps, both paid for on the day.** Assigning the pillar from the old numeric
family alone mis-files every AI video that happened to be coded in a ClickUp family:
five had to be moved to A by hand afterwards, so read the title, not only the number.
And the number is an ID inside the pillar and never a running order, which means it is
also **never renumbered to close a gap** — a gap is what proves nothing was reused.

**This weighting points at the wrong subjects, and it should be raised before the
next batch is planned.** On the nearest positioning twin, identity subjects did 5x
the median while systems, operations and AI tooling sat flat: mapped onto these
codes, C and A are the flat subjects inside the 70% core while L, the identity
pillar, sits in the 30%. The doctrine is the source of truth for pillar shares
and wins over this note, so do not silently rebalance a batch: raise it, get the
share changed there, then code to the new one. Full numbers and caveats in
`vibe-kit/ai-doc/references/competitor-evidence.md`.

**Resolved, 20 Aug 2026.** S and P were reassigned on 30 Jul: S was Seam and is now Skill, P was Passion and is now Product. The leftover Passion cooking shorts have now been rehoused to L, so **P means Product and nothing else**. There were two, not three: `P01 - I run my week the way I cook` is `L10`, `P02 - Mise en place is a project management method` is `L11`, and P03 never existed. P04 and P05 are Product and did not move.
