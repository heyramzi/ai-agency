# Harvest the phrase, do not invent it

The viewer's own words exist in two corpora and neither is the writer's head.

`customer-voice` holds them from 367 recorded calls. The other is the search bar:
`scripts/search-demand.py "<seed keyword>"` reads TikTok's autocomplete index, expands the seed
through a-z and through the shapes a problem gets typed in, and returns the 3-to-6-word phrases
people finish the query with. No login, no key, about two minutes for six seeds.

## File what comes back as a subject first, a title second, a hook third

Hook construction does not separate winners from controls, so a harvested phrase is not a lever on
views. What it is instead is a phrase somebody typed because they wanted the answer, which is
demand evidence, and `idea-mining` owns it.

Read the `from` column and drop the collisions: on a niche channel the seed "ai agent" returns
Valorant and estate agents.

Method single-sourced from Natia Kurdadze, 28 Aug 2026, no control test; the expansion and the
classifier are ours.
