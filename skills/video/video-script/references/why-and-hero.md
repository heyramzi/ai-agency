# Why first, and the viewer as the hero

`SKILL.md` carries the rule. This is the craft under it: which emotional lever is spent where, how
the hero's arc maps onto the nine blocks, and how the why block itself is filled.

The author, 19 Sep 2026, on a Claude guide whose body opened straight into the broken morning:
**"We always should start with why when we record a video."**

## The lever, and where it is spent

- **The lever is fear, and the fear has to be already true.** Name the thing that is broken today,
  load the consequence onto it, then stop and let the viewer answer it. The nine levers and what
  each one has to be true about: `video-hooks`,
  [`register.md`](register.md),
  "The emotional register". The honesty half is that skill's truth rule, and it binds hardest here,
  because an invented stake is the one thing that can actually cost the account.
- **Spend the emotion in three places and nowhere else**: the why, the stakes, and the last beats of
  the proof. A build block is teaching, and `register.md` already bans a threat stacked onto a
  definition. Somebody who came to learn where the button is didn't come to be frightened about
  their own business, and pressure with no release is what makes a walkthrough feel like an ad.
- **One lever per block, named in the brief.** The Claude guide's brief carries the table: fear in
  the why, shame-release in the failure, loss aversion in the stakes, nothing through the four
  loads, identity split at the close of the proof. Writing it down is what stops the draft
  spraying urgency over a walkthrough.

## The arc, mapped onto the blocks

The viewer is the hero and you're the mentor: one scar, one gift, then out of the way.
Creator-as-hero is an announcement. The arc itself is `storytelling`, "The hero's arc"; this is
only which block plays which position.

| Arc position | Block | What it owes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary world, and the call | The why | One object the viewer owns, and the clock on it |
| Refusal, and the ordeal | The failure | They already tried. Your own scar, two beats at most |
| The cost of the refusal | The stakes | What it costs per week, measured, with the artefact |
| The gift | The mechanism | The one portable idea, named once |
| The road of trials | Build 1..n | One failure per block, fixed on camera, idea restated |
| Return changed | The proof | The opening object replayed, changed. Never a bare number |

**The pronoun count is how creator-as-hero gets caught, and `reviewScript` runs it.** "Our proposal
skill takes the CRM record we created" is the mentor doing the work while the hero watches; "your
own record, the one you filed two minutes ago" is the same demo with the hero inside it. Measured on
the 19 Sep 2026 Claude guide: 2 of 10 blocks failed, both of them builds, which is where the drift
always happens, because a demo is narrated by whoever is holding the mouse.

## The why block, beat by beat

Eleven beats at a 174-beat budget, in this order.

- **Where they are now, in one object they own.** The Monday morning, the chat window, the folder
  nobody else can open. That object comes back at the end of the proof, changed, so pick one you can
  film twice.
- **What's already broken, in their words.** Out of `conversion`, quoted rather than
  paraphrased: a phrase 367 recorded calls say is theirs beats a phrase you wrote for them.
- **The consequence, with a clock on it.** Not that it's bad. That it's still true in twelve months,
  and that twelve months is the whole window. This is the one beat allowed to frighten anybody, and
  it needs a real number or a real artefact under it.
- **The two kinds of people, so they pick a side themselves.** Identity split, one sentence, and the
  viewer has to be able to place themselves without being told which side is theirs. Telling them is
  the patronising failure logged 10 Sep 2026 in `learned-patterns.md`.
- **The turn: what separates the two is one decision, named and not yet explained.** That is the
  loop the mechanism block closes, and the only thing this block owes the rest of the video.

Then stop. A why block that starts teaching has spent the mechanism block's material, and the
mechanism block is where the viewer actually gets to keep something.
