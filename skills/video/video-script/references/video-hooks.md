# The opening line

The opening decides whether people **stay**. It does not decide how many arrive.

That distinction was measured across 27 transcripts (Matt Gray) and replicated across 627
transcripts and 13 channels (26 Aug 2026): opening length (107 vs 106 words) and first number
arrival (22.2s vs 23.9s) show zero correlation with view count. A video that underperformed on
views is never fixed here.

**The hook opens the loop. It cannot close it.** What keeps somebody past the open is
`storytelling`: one question opened early and paid off late, the five moves, and the ban on
restating. A hook whose body then restates itself bought attention for nothing.

**The subject decides reach, then the title and thumbnail, and only then is the hook ever heard.**
If a video underperformed on views, do not rewrite the hook: check the subject was worth making
(`idea-mining`), rewrite the title, and regenerate the thumbnail (`youtube-thumbnail`).
`vibe-kit/ai-doc/references/competitor-evidence.md` has the two title levers that survived the
control test. `scripts/teardown.py <url>` prints a reference video's first 30 seconds and its
first-number moment, the two the Matt Gray finding is stated in.

Where this skill earns its keep is everything after the click: the swipe-away window on a Short,
and whether a long-form viewer is still there at ninety seconds, which is retention and what
completion rate measures. The hook still matters enormously; it just is not responsible for a
number it never touched.

**A dip straight after an off-target spike is a cohort problem, not a hook problem.** When a video
reaches far outside the ICP, the next few get served to that borrowed audience and land flat, so do
not rewrite a format that was working. Judge the spike on joins rather than views:
 Hypothesis from a short-form course newsletter, not control-tested here.

**Show the number, do not only say it.** Where a real artefact exists (a dashboard, a counter, a
board, an invoice), the proof frame is that artefact, within two seconds of the claim; the
strongest version is two artefacts from one input with a verdict that splits rather than crowns.
Why, and the source: [proof-and-surfaces.md](proof-and-surfaces.md).

## Point at the viewer, and pick the register

A script addresses the viewer's situation rather than reporting yours, and the emotional register is chosen rather than defaulted. See [register.md](register.md).

**A hook is ultra specific or it is not a hook.** Stated 21 Aug 2026: "the hook needs to be ultra
specific always", and "people need to identify". The viewer has to run the claim against their own
week and come back with yes or no. "Your business does not run without you" is a general truth
nobody answers; "if you run an agency and it stops the week you go on holiday, you are the system"
names a week the viewer either had or did not. The forcing shape: `if you <a situation with a
number, a tool or a person in it>`, then the verdict.
**The situation is found downstream of the topic, never inside it**, and must not reappear in the
payoff: "three clauses every landlord needs in their lease" identifies nobody, "you should not be
unblocking a toilet at 11pm" identifies the same people. How to generate one, scenario injection:
[mechanisms.md](mechanisms.md). It is also the audience filter: a hook
that names the wrong people loses nobody worth keeping, and one that names 28-year-olds in
Minnesota working at Walmart keeps exactly them. Losing the rest of the feed is the point.

**Then stop, because the hook must not answer itself.** Same day, on a hook that named the
situation and delivered the verdict in the same breath: two mechanisms are running and the second
dies on contact with a payoff. Self-reference gets the sentence processed as being about the
viewer (Kelley et al. 2002; Rogers 1977), and the missing main clause leaves it unfinished, which
is what keeps it live (Zeigarnik 1927; Loewenstein's information gap, 1994). Say "if your account
manager spends 2 hours a day updating a sheet" and let the next line, slide or second carry the
verdict.

**An open gap is not enough on its own. It has to look worth closing, and it has to be guessable.**
The two conditions, what separates a hook from bait, and seven open shapes that carry them:
[mechanisms.md](mechanisms.md).
**Harvest the phrase, do not invent it.** `conversion` holds the viewer's own words from 367
recorded calls, and `scripts/search-demand.py` reads what people actually type into the search bar.
Both, and what to file the result as: [harvest.md](harvest.md).
In `humanizer` pattern 45, digits not words for a hook's numbers. The carousel form of it, the
influence ladder across six slides, and why scarcity is deliberately unused: `carousel`,
`references/native-campaigns.md`.

## Three surfaces, and confirm the click

A hook is written three times: the visual in the first frame, the title text on screen, and the
spoken line, and **they have to carry the same claim.** What each surface carries, which one is
optional, the read-order evidence, and the direct-versus-implied contrast rule:
[proof-and-surfaces.md](proof-and-surfaces.md).
Separate from the hook and easy to confuse with it: the title and thumbnail made a promise, and the
open has to show immediately this is the video that promise came from, in the viewer's own words
rather than a restatement of the title (`storytelling` rung 0 bans that). A viewer who clicked on
one subject and hears a different one in the first breath leaves before the hook has finished.
`youtube-thumbnail`, `references/angle.md` records the claim the frame made; that claim is the
debt this line settles.
**Two windows, two jobs**: the 1-to-3-second Shorts window and the 30-second long-form open, with
the measurements behind each: [two-windows.md](two-windows.md).

## The five mechanisms

Shock or contradiction, problem agitation, story open, curiosity gap, social proof. Name the one
before writing, because an unnamed hook usually turns out to be two fighting. How each works, what
it suits and how it fails: [mechanisms.md](mechanisms.md).

## Execution

0. **The open is written out in full; the body it feeds is bullets.** The opening lines are the
   one place where exact wording is load-bearing, so they ship verbatim, marked `Say this line as
   written`. Everything after: the long-form branch, "Every script ships as bullets".
1. **On long-form, write the open last, from the finished transcript.** An open written in advance
   ends up promising the video that was planned rather than the one that exists. Does not apply to
   a Short, where the hook is the plan.
2. **Find the true number first.** If nothing is measured and screenshottable, say so and stop.
3. **Pick the window**, 1 to 3 seconds or 30 seconds, and hold the word budget.
4. **Write at least three variants on different mechanisms.** One variant is not a choice.
5. **Rate each one**: mechanism, word count, drop-off risk with a reason, and what makes it fail.
6. **Read them aloud.** A hook that cannot be said in one breath is not a hook.
7. **Run the voice pass.** Hand the winner to `humanizer` with a sample of the person actually
   speaking. A mechanically correct hook in the wrong voice still loses.
8. Append a new failure mode to Learned Patterns with today's date.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] The first line describes the viewer's problem, not the author's history, and survives the stranger test
- [ ] The first-person count was taken across the whole script and it is zero, or every survivor is a hedge on his own number, the DM ask, an assignment, or a line somebody else said. The hook itself carries none: an opening about the writer spends the only attention the video is guaranteed. `humanizer`, "On camera, every I is a you"
- [ ] The closing line points at the viewer's next action, not what the author did, every middle beat carries a consequence rather than a definition, and the close is a command rather than a recap
- [ ] An emotional lever was chosen by name before the hook was written, and the hook is not merely informative
- [ ] The subject was checked against harvested search demand rather than against what the writer assumed the viewer calls it
- [ ] The gap was tested for payoff, not only suspense ("worth knowing", not just "want to know"), and it is guessable: a viewer can form a wrong prediction, rather than only knowing an answer is being withheld
- [ ] On a Short, the stakes carry all three parts (a character, something at risk, a clock), and every re-hook survived having its transition deleted with a fact or a question still standing
- [ ] Every number in the hook is measured, not estimated, and is traceable to a source or a screen
- [ ] The first frame shows the proof where a real artefact exists, and any A/B claim puts both artefacts on screen from the same input
- [ ] Any counted promise in the open is paid by that many named, countable beats in the body
- [ ] The opening names a moment the viewer has lived rather than the subject the video teaches, and that moment is absent from the payoff
- [ ] On a counted list, the item that carries the result is ordered last, so the demotion before it states a true ranking rather than a transition
- [ ] All three hook surfaces were written, and the visual, the title text and the spoken line carry one claim rather than three
- [ ] Where the mechanism is contrast, the common belief is stated on screen or out loud before it is broken
- [ ] On long-form, the first five seconds name the subject the title promised in the viewer's words, the open was written against the recorded transcript rather than the plan, and past thirty minutes it answers the safe beat
- [ ] Word count is inside the budget for the chosen window
- [ ] Three or more variants were written on different named mechanisms, each carrying a drop-off risk rating with a reason
- [ ] The winner survived being read aloud in one breath, and went through `humanizer` against a real speech sample
- [ ] Negation pivots were counted: at most one in the script, short and concrete
- [ ] `npx heyramzi-slop <file> --fix` exits 0. Fix what it names, run it again, and do not ship on a non-zero exit
- [ ] Any new failure mode was appended to Learned Patterns
